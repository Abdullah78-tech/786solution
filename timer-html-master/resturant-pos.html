Advanced POS & Inventory Tracking

🍽️ Run your restaurant smarter with 786 Solution's Restaurant POS Software!

Whether you own a café, a bustling restaurant, or a growing food chain, our robust Odoo-based POS helps you manage everything from a single, unified system. We have designed this software to eliminate paperwork, reduce human error, and accelerate your service speed.

Dine-In Orders & Table Reservations

Seamlessly book tables in advance and manage walk-in customers. Our intuitive interface allows your staff to quickly assign parties to tables, avoiding double-bookings and minimizing customer wait times.

Take Away & Home Delivery

Handle off-premises orders with ease. Track delivery rider status, manage specific packaging requirements, and ensure that delivery times are closely monitored for maximum customer satisfaction.

Visual Floor Management

Map out your restaurant's exact layout digitally. Your staff can easily monitor table status (available, occupied, billed), transfer tickets between tables, or split bills directly from the floor plan.

Professional Custom Billing

Generate highly professional, customized receipts featuring your restaurant's logo and contact details. Support for multiple payment methods, split payments, and fast checkout keeps the line moving.

Live Kitchen Order Tracking (KOT)

Send orders directly from the POS to the Kitchen Display System (KDS) or kitchen printers instantly. This reduces miscommunications, improves food prep times, and keeps front-of-house staff updated.

Cash Flow & Inventory Management

Keep a tight grip on your finances and stock levels. Track daily ingredient consumption, manage vendor payments, and set up low-stock alerts so you never run out of your best-selling items.

End-of-Day Sales & Reports

Automate your daily closing process. Generate comprehensive analytics detailing sales by category, peak hours, cashier shift summaries, and profit margins with just a single click.

No more paperwork. No more confusion. Just one powerful system for your entire restaurant.
