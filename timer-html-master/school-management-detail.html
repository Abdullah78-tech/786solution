Complete School ERP Solution

🎓 Unified Portal for Staff, Students & Administrators

Designed as an all-in-one platform for educational institutions, our School Management System eliminates the need for separate software for admissions, fees, and HR. From the initial setup of academic sessions to the daily management of school operations, this system handles it all.

Real-Time Dashboard

Get a bird's-eye view of your school's operations instantly. Track key performance indicators including total enrolled students, daily attendance, fee collections, outstanding dues, and expense summaries.

Student Registration & Admission

Streamline the enrollment process. Easily create new student profiles, link parent/guardian details, upload required documents, and process admissions with single-click bulk enrollment.

Fee Challans & Payment Tracking

Automate your financial workflow. Generate monthly or admission fee challans, apply custom fee structures and concessions, register student payments, and automatically maintain a defaulters list.

Smart Attendance Management

Maintain precise daily records. Mark students as Present, Absent, Late, or on Leave with optional remarks. Generate confirmed attendance registers and view comprehensive monthly summaries.

Examinations & Promotions

Automate academic assessments. Create exams in bulk, define subjects and passing criteria, enter student marks, generate professional report cards, and automatically process student promotions.

HR, Staff & Payroll

Manage your employees seamlessly alongside your students. Track staff attendance with check-in/out records, configure salary structures, and automatically generate monthly payroll and payslips.

General Expenses & Configuration

Configure academic sessions, ID cards, and exam terms before the year begins. Log daily school expenses by category, memo, and payment method to ensure your school remains financially healthy.

Replace manual registers and scattered spreadsheets with one unified, powerful platform.
