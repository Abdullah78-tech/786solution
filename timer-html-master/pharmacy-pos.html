Secure POS with Expiry & Batch Tracking

Simplify your daily operations with our modern, intelligent Pharmacy Management System. Built specifically for pharmacies and medical stores, this platform empowers you to manage medicines, sales, purchases, and prescriptions while ensuring absolute inventory accuracy.

Built for your business

Everything you need, in one place

Batch & Lot Number Management

Maintain total compliance and quality control. The system ensures every medicine is stored and tracked using its unique batch and lot number, making product recalls and audits effortless.

Rack & Shelf Location Tracking

Find medicines instantly during busy hours. By saving the specific rack and shelf location of every product in the database, you can dramatically reduce search time and serve patients faster.

Expiry Date & Low Stock Alerts

Never dispense expired medications again. Our software proactively tracks shelf life and live stock quantities, sending automated alerts for impending expiries and items running low on stock.

Barcode Scanning & Fast Billing

Speed up the checkout process to keep lines moving. Support rapid barcode scanning for immediate pricing retrieval, ensuring high-speed billing and zero manual entry errors at the counter.

Prescription & Customer Records

Build trust with a secure database. Safely record patient details, store digital prescriptions, and track purchase histories to provide personalized service and streamline repeat orders.

Purchase & Supplier Management

Optimize your supply chain. Easily track incoming shipments, process purchase orders, handle damaged returns, and manage all supplier communication directly from a centralized dashboard.

Analytics & Secure Cloud Backup

Make data-driven decisions with detailed sales, profit, and inventory reports. Assign multi-user access with specific roles and rely on secure cloud backups to keep your pharmacy data safe.
